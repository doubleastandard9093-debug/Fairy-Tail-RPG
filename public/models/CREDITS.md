## Currently unused (kept, not deleted)
These were copied with the set but are not placed yet, because they don't fit Fiore's temperate setting around Magnolia: `nature/cactus_*`, `nature/palm_*`, `nature/dead_*`, and everything in `props/` (fences, crates, campfires, pillars, stalls, cart). Palms could suit Hargeon's coast or Akane later. They stay in the repo until a placement is approved, or until the owner approves deleting them.
`nature/autumn_*` is used: in autumn, it replaces half of the broadleaf trees.
